For anyone comparing clinics: Luxe Aura's four main devices, what they actually do, and which treatments use them.

01First in Sumatra

Onda Pro

DEKA, Italy

Coolwaves microwave energy for localised fat, cellulite and loose skin on the body, without surgery.

  • Fat cells absorb its energy before any other tissue does, so it works in the right layer without harming the surface of the skin.
  • The handpiece is cooled. The skin feels warm, not hot, while the layer beneath is treated.
  • One device for three concerns: stubborn fat, cellulite and loose skin on the stomach, thighs and arms.

Used for

  • Onda Pro
  • Lymphatic

02First in Sumatra

RedTouch Pro

DEKA, Italy

A 675 nm laser that works directly on collagen for texture, pores, acne scars and fine lines.

  • The 675 nm wavelength is absorbed selectively by collagen. Earlier lasers had to injure the surface to reach it.
  • Because the skin surface is not damaged, the risk of post-laser darkening, common on Asian skin, is far smaller.
  • Redness clears within hours. It fits into a lunch break.

Used for

  • RedTouch Pro

03First in Sumatra

Curejet

A needle-free injector: actives are driven into the skin under high pressure through a micro opening.

  • No needle enters the skin, which suits anyone who dislikes needles or does not want puncture marks.
  • The pressure itself stimulates the skin to regenerate, so the effect comes from more than what goes in.
  • The doctor chooses the active for your concern: vitamins, hyaluronic acid or a blend.

Used for

  • Curejet

04

Ultraformer MPT

Classys, South Korea

The latest generation of HIFU to lift the cheeks, define the jawline and tighten the neck, with no needles.

  • Focused ultrasound is delivered to the face's supporting layer, a depth that usually only surgery reaches.
  • MPT mode moves more smoothly and faster than older HIFU, so discomfort drops sharply and no anaesthetic is needed.
  • Results build over 2 to 3 months as new collagen forms, and last around a year.

Used for

  • Ultraformer MPT
