01

How it works

DNA Salmon (PDRN) is a DNA fragment from salmon cells whose structure is so close to human DNA that the body accepts it without a rejection response.

Injected into the shallow layer of the skin, PDRN acts like a repair signal: it encourages new cell growth, small blood vessel formation and calms inflammation.

Suited to skin that is often red, acne marks that are slow to heal, and tired skin that needs to be healthy again, not just glowy for an evening.

02

When you will see the result

Skin feels calmer and brighter after 1 to 2 weeks. Usually 3 sessions, 2 to 4 weeks apart, for a result that lasts.

03

Who should not have this treatment

  • Pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Fish or seafood allergy
  • Active skin infection on the face
