01

How it works

RedTouch Pro is a 675 nm laser, a wavelength absorbed directly by collagen. It works in the dermis without having to injure the surface the way ablative lasers do.

Collagen heated in a controlled way triggers repair: old fibres are reorganised, new ones form. Pores tighten, shallow acne scars soften, fine lines blur.

Because the surface is not damaged, it also suits Asian skin, which darkens easily after conventional lasers.

02

When you will see the result

Texture starts to feel smoother after the first session. The best result usually comes after 3 to 4 sessions a month apart, and keeps improving for a few months after that.

03

Who should not have this treatment

  • Pregnancy
  • Sunburnt or recently tanned skin
  • Photosensitising medication (such as isotretinoin) in the last 6 months
  • Active skin infection on the face
